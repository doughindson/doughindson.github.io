# Chase — US Open

*Role:* Art Director

*Studio:* BUCK

*Client:* Chase

Chase has a longstanding partnership with the US Open tennis tournament, and they came to BUCK looking for a spot that would celebrate this partnership, their financial tools, and the great city of New York.

Each scene was a love letter to the iconic sport of tennis, and iconic scenes from New York, where the tournament is held. From the brownstones of Queens, neighbourhood courts, and all the way to the Arthur Ashe stadium.

Inspired by the essential simplicity of the blue and green courts in the stadium, we kept the colour palette limited and shapes intentionally graphic and refined. We brought a sense of realism through rich lighting and dynamic camera angles — channeling the energy of the tournament itself.
