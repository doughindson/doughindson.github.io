# Thorne — Build to Last

[Video — video-01-f0534023-a74d-4c8f-990d-14b6462c500e.mp4 (Squarespace-hosted, 46s)](./thorne-build-to-last/video-01-f0534023-a74d-4c8f-990d-14b6462c500e.mp4)

*Role:* Art Director

*Studio:* BUCK

*Client:* Thorne

BUCK partnered with Thorne on Build to Last: an evocative, story-driven, global campaign that draws parallels between Thorne and Dwyane & Zaire Wades' pursuit of excellence.

The final assets were used across a broad array of placements, from socials and web to OOH. That required a robust design system that made use of the unique layouts in each location.

Alongside the live-action, we also shot stills of each of the key products on location, grounding them in real world, active situations.

A combination of serif, sans-serif and monospace type allowed for a range of expression, and paired perfectly with the cinematic grading of the films and photography.
