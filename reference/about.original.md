# Doug Hindson

## Creative Director Art Director Designer etc.

Oslo, Norway

(+47) 412 66 789
[hello@doughindson.com](mailto:hello@doughindson.com)

Find me on [Vimeo](http://www.vimeo.com/doughindson) & [Instagram](http://www.instagram.com/doughindson).

*All work © Doug Hindson unless otherwise stated.*

My aim is to bring a thoughtful approach to whatever it is that I tackle; creativity with a strong sense of design and an emphasis on concept and storytelling. Whether that be through design, animation, illustration, photography or carving a spoon from a slab of oak.

Currently leading the design department at [NewsLab](https://www.newslab.no/) in Oslo, across a wide range of design, illustration and motion projects.

Before that I was an Associate Creative Director at [Buck](https://buck.co) in NYC for four and a half years, and spent five years in London, where I had the pleasure of working with companies such as [Studio AKA](https://www.studioaka.co.uk), [Chromosphere LA](http://chromosphere-la.com), [Nexus Studios](https://nexusstudios.com), and [Google Creative Lab](http://www.creativelab5.com/) among others.

My work has been shown at festivals across the world and I'm the proud owner of a [D&AD Award](https://www.dandad.org/awards/new-blood/2014/dcm-show-the-emotion-of-film/2433/frisson/), [Vimeo Staff Pick](https://vimeo.com/116533375) and was a finalist for the [ADC Young Guns](https://www.shootonline.com/news/lineup-global-finalists-set-one-clubs-young-guns-19).

Graduated with First Class Honours in Illustration & Animation BA(Hons) at Kingston University 2014.
